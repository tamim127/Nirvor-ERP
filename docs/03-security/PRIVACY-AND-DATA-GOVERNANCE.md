# Privacy and Data Governance

## Principles

- Collect only data needed for defined business purposes.
- Classify data by sensitivity.
- Define retention and deletion rules by data category.
- Provide tenant-controlled export where appropriate.
- Keep audit records protected from ordinary tenant editing.
- Restrict platform support access.
- Do not use tenant data for unrelated purposes without a valid basis and transparent policy.
- Separate backups from live deletion workflows and define expiry.
- Document subprocessors and external data flows.

## Data categories

- Identity and contact data
- Employee and HR data
- Financial and tax data
- Customer and supplier records
- Operational records
- Uploaded documents
- Security and audit logs
- Usage and billing metadata

## Decisions requiring legal review

- Applicable Bangladesh privacy, cybersecurity, tax, labor, and record-retention obligations
- Cross-border processing
- Data processing agreements
- Data subject/customer requests
- Breach notification obligations
- Retention periods for accounting and employment records

This document is an engineering baseline, not legal advice.
