# Personas and Jobs to Be Done

## Purpose

Describe user needs without assuming that every company behaves the same way. Validate these personas through interviews and workflow observation.

## Persona A: Small business owner

Needs:
- A simple overview of sales, cash, stock, and outstanding invoices.
- Minimal setup and affordable pricing.
- A small number of users and clear permissions.
- Easy mobile access.

Success: Can understand the business position and complete common tasks without specialist training.

## Persona B: Growing SME operations manager

Needs:
- Multiple departments and branches.
- Standardized approvals.
- Inventory and procurement visibility.
- Reliable reports and accountability.

Success: Can coordinate teams and detect operational exceptions.

## Persona C: Finance manager

Needs:
- Accurate ledgers and traceable transactions.
- Controlled posting and period closing.
- Reconciliation and audit trails.
- Exportable financial reports.

Success: Can trust reported balances and explain how each balance was produced.

## Persona D: Enterprise administrator

Needs:
- Multiple companies, branches, and complex permission policies.
- Central governance with delegated administration.
- Integration and audit controls.
- Predictable performance and support.

Success: Can govern the group without removing local operational autonomy.

## Persona E: Employee

Needs:
- A focused workspace showing relevant tasks.
- Mobile-friendly attendance, requests, approvals, and notifications.
- Clear status and next action.

Success: Can complete assigned work quickly without navigating irrelevant modules.

## Research questions

- What tools and spreadsheets are used today?
- Which workflows create the most errors or delays?
- Who approves each transaction?
- Which reports are needed daily, weekly, monthly, and annually?
- What must work on a phone?
- What data must never be editable after posting?
- What integrations are essential?
- What would make a customer switch or cancel?
